# MalayMenu

[MalayMenu](https://malaymenu.my/) compares restaurant menu prices across Malaysia, helping diners find good value before they order, whether they are eating out with family, grabbing lunch at work, or planning a treat at the weekend.

The site covers seven everyday categories: Fast Food, Coffee & Drinks, Asian, Japanese, Pizza, Western, and Desserts. Within each category you can browse familiar chains and local favourites side by side, compare similar dishes, and spot the cheapest option for the meal you want.

Popular names featured include McDonald's, KFC, Starbucks, Tealive, Nando's, and Secret Recipe, alongside many other Malaysian restaurants. Prices are presented in a clear, simple format, making it easy to check a burger, a coffee, a set meal, or a whole family feast without visiting several different websites. Menus are updated regularly, so the figures you see should reflect current prices, although individual outlets may vary.

Use [MalayMenu](https://malaymenu.my/) to plan your next meal, compare prices across outlets, and choose the option that suits your taste and your budget.
